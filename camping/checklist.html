Group Camping Checklist

  • Your Gear
  • Group Gear
  • Shared Items

Use this list to prepare for your group camping trip. Items are broken down by what each person needs to bring, what the group will provide, and what can be shared.

Personal Essentials

  • Backpack (sized for your gear)
  • Sleeping bag (rated for the expected temperatures)
  • Sleeping pad or air mattress
  • Headlamp or flashlight with extra batteries
  • First-aid kit (personal medications and basic supplies)
  • Toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, towel, etc.)
  • Sunscreen and bug repellent
  • Camp chair or stool
  • Snacks for hiking and personal cravings

Clothing

Layers are key to staying comfortable in changing conditions.

  • Moisture-wicking base layers (synthetics or wool)
  • Insulating mid-layer (fleece or down jacket)
  • Waterproof and windproof outer shell
  • Hiking boots or sturdy shoes
  • Extra socks (wool or synthetic)
  • Hat (for sun or warmth) and gloves

Will Be Made Available to the Group

  • Utensils, bowls and plates for eating
  • Cooking gear (stove, fuel, lighter/matches)
  • Cooking supplies (spices, oil, etc.)
  • Food (Raw ingredients will be purchased locally)
  • Water bottles (a case of drinking water bottles will be available)
  • Cooler for perishable food items courtesy of Valdir
  • Trash bags for waste disposal
  • Multi-tool or knife
  • Map and compass or GPS device
  • Group first-aid kit (additional supplies)
  • Rope or paracord for various uses
  • Firewood (if allowed and not provided)

Recommended to Bring and Share

  • Lantern for group lighting
  • Games or entertainment (cards, board games, etc.)
  • Portable power bank for charging devices
