Group Camping Checklist

This list will help you prepare for a fun group camping trip!

Personal Essentials

  • Backpack (sized for your gear)
  • Sleeping bag (rated for the expected temperatures)
  • Sleeping pad or air mattress
  • Headlamp or flashlight with extra batteries
  • First-aid kit (personal medications and basic supplies)
  • Toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, Towel, etc.)
  • Sunscreen and bug repellent
  • Camp chair or stool

Clothing

Layers are key to staying comfortable in changing conditions.

  • Moisture-wicking base layers (synthetics or wool)
  • Insulating mid-layer (fleece or down jacket)
  • Waterproof and windproof outer shell
  • Hiking boots or sturdy shoes
  • Extra socks (wool or synthetic)
  • Hat (for sun or warmth) and gloves

Will be made available to the group

  • Snacks for hiking and personal cravings
  • Personal reusable cup, plate, and cutlery
  • Small towel (quick-drying is best)
  • Camp chair or stool
  • Garbage bags to pack out all trash
