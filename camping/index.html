Packing list

Here's a suggested packing list to help you prepare for our camping trip. Feel free to customize it based on your personal needs and preferences!

  • Tent (if not provided by the group)
  • Sleeping bag and sleeping pad
  • Camping pillow
  • Clothing suitable for the weather (layers, rain gear, etc.)
    *Always bring a hoodie and sweatpants (warm clothes) to sit outside in case it get cold.
  • Hiking boots or sturdy shoes
  • 1 or 2 extra socks and underwear
  • Hat and sunglasses
  • Swimsuit and towel (if you plan to swim)
  • Personal toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, etc.)
    *Bring a pair of flipflops to go shower
  • Insect repellent and sunscreen
  • Water bottle or hydration system
  • Flashlight or headlamp with extra batteries
    *A headlamp is preferred so you can have your hands free
    *Preferably with red light mode helps keep you from blinding those around you and keep bugs from flying to your face
  • Personal medications and first aid kit
  • Snacks and food for personal meals
  • Camping chair or seating pad
  • Camera or smartphone for photos
  • Bycicle and riding gear (if you plan to ride with the group)
  • Optional: Books, games, or other entertainment

* The bycicles will be loaded on Hamdi and Alina's car. There are 9 projected campers and 10 bycycle spots. So it should all go smoothly.
* There are free showers and bathrooms throughout the reservation. There will be one very close to our camp site
